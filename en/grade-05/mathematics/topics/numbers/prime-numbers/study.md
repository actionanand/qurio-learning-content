# Prime Numbers

A prime number has exactly **two positive factors**:

- $1$
- the number itself

Examples:

$$
2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 13
$$

The number $2$ is the only even prime number.

A number with more than two positive factors is called a **composite number**.

For example, $12$ has factors $1,2,3,4,6,12$, so it is composite.
