# Factorial

Factorial is a compact way to multiply whole numbers down to $1$.

The symbol is **!**.

$$
5! = 5 \times 4 \times 3 \times 2 \times 1 = 120
$$

Similarly:

$$
3! = 3 \times 2 \times 1 = 6
$$

By definition:

$$
0! = 1
$$

> This is an enrichment topic for learners who want extra number practice.
