# Probability

Probability tells us **how likely** an event is to happen.

For simple equally likely outcomes:

$$
P(\text{event}) = \frac{\text{favourable outcomes}}{\text{total outcomes}}
$$

## Example

A fair coin has two possible outcomes: Head and Tail.

$$
P(\text{Head}) = \frac{1}{2}
$$

For a standard die, the probability of rolling an even number is:

$$
P(\text{even}) = \frac{3}{6} = \frac{1}{2}
$$

## Remember

- Probability is between $0$ and $1$.
- $0$ means impossible.
- $1$ means certain.
