# Continents and Oceans

Earth has **seven continents** and **five commonly recognized oceans**.

## Seven continents

1. Asia
2. Africa
3. North America
4. South America
5. Antarctica
6. Europe
7. Australia

## Five oceans

1. Pacific Ocean
2. Atlantic Ocean
3. Indian Ocean
4. Southern Ocean
5. Arctic Ocean

The **Pacific Ocean** is the largest ocean, and **Asia** is the largest continent by land area.
